## Research question

How do culture conditions and induction relate to biological responses during investigation of a heterologous microbial biosynthetic gene cluster?

## Context

This research concerns the palmerolide biosynthetic gene cluster and its study in *Aeromonas salmonicida* subsp. *pectinolytica* 34mel. The project brings together microbial physiology, molecular biology, and gene-expression analysis.

## Approach

The ongoing work examines culture and induction conditions alongside molecular readouts, including RT-qPCR and RNA-seq analysis. These methods provide complementary ways to investigate biological responses; this page does not report experimental outcomes.

## Scope and status

This is ongoing research. No claim of successful palmerolide production or other experimental result is made here. Detailed protocols, unpublished data, and collaborator information are intentionally not included.
