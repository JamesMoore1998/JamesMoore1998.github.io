## Focus

Transcriptomic data can help connect experimental conditions to biological responses. This project area focuses on building careful, reproducible analysis practices that support interpretation rather than replacing it.

## Analytical questions

Topics of interest include data quality, differential gene expression, visualization, experimental design, and the limits of statistical inference. The specific analyses and datasets shared publicly will depend on verification and research approval.

## Current status

This is an area of developing computational work. No dataset, analysis result, or claim of a completed RNA-seq pipeline is presented on this page.
